# Business Questions and Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

### Questions
- What is the total sales amount?
- Which month has the highest sales?
- Which month has the lowest sales?
- How do sales change month over month?

## 2. Customer Analysis

### Questions
- Which gender contributes more to sales?
- Which age group contributes the most sales?
- Which age group has the highest order volume?

## 3. Product Analysis

### Questions
- Which category generates the highest sales?
- Which category has the highest quantity sold?
- Which products contribute most to sales?

## 4. Sales Channel Analysis

### Questions
- Which sales channel generates the highest sales?
- Which channel has the highest order volume?
- How do Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart compare?

## 5. Geographic Analysis

### Questions
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- What are the top 5 states by sales?
- Does the highest-sales state also have the highest order volume?

## 6. Order Status Analysis

### Questions
- What percentage of records are delivered?
- How many records are returned?
- How many are cancelled?
- How many are refunded?

## 7. Business Insights

Insights will be added after validating each analytical question using the Excel data and PivotTables.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Recommendations will be based on the validated findings from the analysis.
