# Business Questions and Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

### Q1. What is the total sales amount?

**Answer:** ₹21,176,377

**Insight:** The dataset records approximately ₹2.12 crore in sales during 2022.

---

### Q2. Which month has the highest sales?

**Answer:** March — ₹1,928,066

**Insight:** March was the strongest sales month.

---

### Q3. Which month has the lowest sales?

**Answer:** November — ₹1,615,356

**Insight:** November recorded the lowest monthly sales.

---

### Q4. How do sales change throughout the year?

**Answer:** Sales increased from January to March, followed by a general decline with some fluctuations. March was the peak, while November was the lowest month.

**Insight:** Monthly performance should be investigated further by channel and product category to understand the reasons for these changes.

---

# 7. Overall Business Insights

1. March recorded the highest monthly sales.
2. November recorded the lowest monthly sales.
3. Women contributed the majority of recorded sales.
4. Adults were the highest-sales age group.
5. Set was the highest-sales product category.
6. Amazon was the leading sales channel.
7. Maharashtra was the highest-sales state.
8. Sales were concentrated in a few major product categories and channels.

---

# 8. Business Recommendations

1. Investigate why March generated the highest sales and whether specific categories or channels drove the increase.
2. Analyze the decline in sales during the later months, especially November.
3. Study the products and categories driving women's sales.
4. Investigate the strong performance of the Set category.
5. Compare Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart by category and month.
6. Investigate sales performance in the top five states.
7. Analyze returned and cancelled records by category and channel.
8. Standardize state names before performing further geographic analysis.

---

# 9. Data Limitations

- Multiple rows can have the same Order ID.
- Record counts are not necessarily unique order counts.
- State names should be standardized before geographic analysis.
- Sales amount does not represent profit because cost information is not available.
- The dataset covers one year, so year-over-year growth cannot be calculated.
